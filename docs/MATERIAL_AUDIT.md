## Dataset mismatch and primary task

The primary task remains three-class lung histology (`lung_n`, `lung_aca`, `lung_scc`) because that is the task in the supplied article and notebook. The Streamlit ZIP contains a separate binary CT-scan classifier and a tabular risk model. Their model weights, labels, images, predictions, and results are not used in the histology prototype.

The GeeksforGeeks article describes a custom subset of approximately 2,400 images and links to a Google Drive folder. The team confirms that the three supplied class archives came from that linked folder. Their naming convention suggests possible derivation from LC25000, whose paper identifies its six authors, but neither the article nor the Drive archives confirm that link or identify who assembled this 2,400-image subset. No explicit license is supplied, and the LC25000 paper's availability statement is not an explicit license. Reuse/redistribution rights remain unverified. There are no reliable patient/slide/source-image identifiers in the supplied files. See `docs/PROVENANCE_AND_GROUP_ID_REVIEW.md` for the evidence and limitations.
